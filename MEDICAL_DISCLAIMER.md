**IMPORTANT: READ CAREFULLY BEFORE USING, DEPLOYING, OR EVALUATING THIS SOFTWARE.**

## 1. Non-Device & Non-Diagnostic Status
- **Not a Certified Medical Device**: MedOrqis is an open-source clinical informatics research, reference architecture, and educational orchestration framework. It has **NOT** been cleared, approved, CE-marked, or certified as Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA), European Medicines Agency / EU Notified Bodies under EU MDR 2017/745, Egyptian Drug Authority (EDA), Saudi Food and Drug Authority (SFDA), or any other national or regional regulatory authority.
- **No Autonomous Diagnostic or Treatment Authority**: MedOrqis does not autonomously diagnose diseases, prescribe medications, modify treatment plans, or triage patients without qualified professional human oversight.

## 2. Intended User Roles & Regulatory Boundaries
### 2.1 For Healthcare Professionals (HCPs)
- MedOrqis is designed strictly as non-autonomous **Clinical Decision Support (CDS)** pursuant to the transparency principles set forth in Section 520(o)(1)(E) of the FD&C Act and the [FDA Clinical Decision Support Software Guidance (January 2026)](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/clinical-decision-support-software) Criterion 4.
- The licensed clinician remains solely, independently, and legally responsible for evaluating, verifying, confirming, and executing any clinical assessment, diagnostic workup, calculator score, dosing recommendation, or guideline citation.
- Never rely on MedOrqis outputs to override clinical judgement, hospital protocols, or direct patient examinations.

### 2.2 For Patients, Consumers & Lay Users
- Outputs provided in Patient-Safe Mode are strictly for **health literacy, educational explanations, and emergency triage redirection**.
- MedOrqis does **NOT** provide personal medical advice, personalized physician consultations, or definitive clinical diagnoses.
- If you are experiencing a medical emergency, severe chest pain, difficulty breathing, sudden neurological deficits, severe allergic reactions, or acute psychiatric distress, **immediately call your local emergency service (e.g., 911, 123 in Egypt, 997 in Saudi Arabia, 112 in the EU/UK) or proceed to the nearest emergency department**.
